A001 high cross_review_disagreement · explicit_conflict · 3 reviewers

Validity of the empirical evidence section

comparison axisValidity of the empirical evidence section as support for the proposal
relation · aspectexplicit_conflict · factual_interpretation

All three reviewers acknowledge the paper uses empirical analyses on ICLR data. Reviewer v6RF asserts the entire empirical evidence section is useless because pretraining-data contamination makes LLM scores on ICLR 2023-2024 data unreliable. Reviewers 156R and Fff5 identify weaknesses (lack of error bars, correlational nature, circularity) but still see the evidence as providing some level of support for the proposal. The disagreement hinges on whether contamination actually invalidates the analyses, and whether the other weaknesses are independently fatal.

三位 reviewer 的立场
review_v6RFstatus=addressed

The empirical evidence section is completely useless due to pretraining-data contamination.

review 中的支撑依据
  • Anything to do with their LLM scores on the ICLR 2023 and 2024 data is completely useless due to pretraining-data contamination.
  • Why are the ICLR 2023 and 2024 data great but ICLR 2025 suddenly much worse? This only makes sense if the pre-training data is already contaminated.
review_156Rstatus=addressed

The empirical evidence has some issues but is not useless; it provides support though needs improvement.

review 中的支撑依据
  • The reviewer is not fully convinced that Figure 2a illustrates the claimed problem.
  • Some empirical figures would be stronger with error bars, reliability estimates, or correlation statistics.
  • The paper is clearly written and supports its position with empirical analyses.
review_Fff5status=addressed

The empirical evidence is correlational and has circularity concerns, but still provides some support.

review 中的支撑依据
  • The analysis is correlational rather than causal.
  • There may be time-varying confounders.
  • The simulation may suffer from model circularity because proxy reviewer and anchor scores use related model families.
需要 AC 核实
  1. Is there evidence that the LLM used for anchor scores was pre-trained on ICLR 2023-2024 data (papers or reviews) prior to the analysis?
  2. If contamination exists, does it affect distributional comparisons or only absolute scores, and can the analysis be replicated with a model known to have no contamination?
  3. What explains the v6RF observation that ICLR 2025 data is ‘much worse’? Could this be a sign of contamination effects?

decision impactIf the contamination claim is substantiated and cannot be mitigated, the paper’s empirical evidence collapses, making a strong case for rejection. If the claim is unsubstantiated or can be addressed (e.g., by using a different LLM or showing robustness), the paper retains its empirical grounding, and the remaining weaknesses may be handled in revision, potentially leading to accept.

对这条 review 打分

四个维度对齐 CHART 实际检查的轴：具体程度、分数与理由一致性、论文证据支撑、专业性。作者的打分是一条独立信号，不参与上面的 anchor 计算，也不会改写 reviewer 的分数。

理由是否具体到可以据此修改论文？1 = 全是笼统措辞　·　5 = 每条都能定位到具体位置
给出的分数与文字理由是否一致？1 = 明显不一致　·　5 = 完全一致
评价是否指向论文里的具体内容？1 = 没有引用任何论文内容　·　5 = 处处有据
表述是否专业、具建设性？1 = 不专业　·　5 = 专业且具建设性
scope为什么这一块单独标注：CHART 当前版本里没有任何作者侧字段。ac_validation 收的是 AC 的判断，followup 收的是 reviewer 的选择，两者都不含作者。这个界面演示的是一个尚未实现的接入点，不是已有能力。
页面演示的边界： 不联网 不上传 review 不需要 API key 不代替人工判断 打分不回传

四类问题，都以能被反驳的形式写出来。

每一项都指向 review 里的具体位置，说明缺什么、和什么冲突。发现 mismatch 不等于判定 reviewer 错了。

门控只有两个出口

两个 specialist 由 planner 调度完毕后，结论要先过 reliability gate。不通过时失败原因会写回上下文让模型重新诊断；若始终不通过，记录标记为 degraded，而不是悄悄输出一份看起来完整的结论。完整流程见 架构图。

结构借自临床病历，不是借自打分器。

医生不会只写一个最终判断，而是把主观陈述、可观察证据、专业评估和后续处理分开记录。CHART 把 SOAP 这套记录逻辑迁移到评审校准上。
