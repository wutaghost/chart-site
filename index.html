评分和理由脱节时，先把落差记录清楚。

CHART 把文字理由、论文证据、rubric 约束和提交分数组织成一份结构化记录，供 reviewer 和 AC 人工检查。

A003 high single_review_concern · under_supported_claim · review_v6RF

Unsupported decisive claim in review review_v6RF

review 里的原始判断The entire Empirical Evidence section is useless because the LLM scores on ICLR 2023/2024 data are contaminated by pretraining data.

Review review_v6RF uses this claim as a material reason for its assessment, but the review does not provide enough checkable support to establish its scope or decision weight.

需要 AC 核实
  1. Which specific LLMs were used and their training data cutoffs.
  2. Evidence that ICLR 2023/2024 papers were included in those models’ pretraining corpora.
  3. Quantitative indication of score inflation on those years vs. uncontaminated data.
  4. Explanation of why contamination fully invalidates the results rather than merely biasing them.
  5. If verified, how much of the overall recommendation depends on it?

decision impactIf the claim is substantiated and materially affects the paper’s main contribution, it may justify the review’s recommendation; if it remains unsupported or narrow in scope, it should not carry decisive weight.

A001 medium single_review_concern · unexplained_rubric_weight · review_gr3d

Unexplained rubric weighting in review review_gr3d

review 里的原始判断The overall score of 5.0 is markedly higher than all dimension scores (support, significance, argument_clarity, related_work all 3; discussion_potential 4), suggesting discussion_potential received decisive weight without explanation for why it alone drove a high overall.

Review review_gr3d appears to let discussion_potential control the recommendation without explaining why that dimension should outweigh the remaining assessment.

需要 AC 核实
  1. Which rubric dimension is intended to be decisive for the recommendation?
  2. Why should that dimension outweigh the other scored dimensions in this case?

decision impactIf the dominant dimension is explicitly decisive under the venue rubric, the recommendation may be coherent; otherwise the AC may need to rebalance the review against the other dimensions.

门控只有两个出口

三个 specialist 由 planner 调度完毕后，结论要先过 reliability gate。不通过时失败原因会写回上下文让模型重新诊断；若始终不通过，记录标记为 degraded，而不是悄悄输出一份看起来完整的结论。完整流程见 架构图。

结构借自临床病历，不是借自打分器。

医生不会只写一个最终判断，而是把主观陈述、可观察证据、专业评估和后续处理分开记录。CHART 把 SOAP 这套记录逻辑迁移到评审校准上。

先用 fixture 跑一遍，再决定要不要接自己的数据。

安装依赖后加上 --mock 就能跑完整流程，不需要 API key、不联网、不消耗 token。上面展示的那条记录换成真实 provider 只需要去掉 --mock 并补上 --paper-pdf。

# 1. 安装依赖，Python 3.10-3.12
$ pip install -r requirements.txt

# 2. 离线跑通 fixture
$ python scripts/run_pipeline.py \
    --txt data/fixtures/review_v6RF.txt \
    --venue icml --mock

# 3. 换成真实 provider 与论文 PDF
$ python scripts/run_pipeline.py \
    --txt data/fixtures/review_156R.txt \
    --paper-pdf data/fixtures/LLMScoring1_paper.pdf \
    --venue icml

  • provider 通过 CHART_PROVIDER 与 CHART_MODEL 配置。
